1. Echo

at The Breakers · Palm Beach

Pan-Asian · Thai · Chinese · Japanese · Korean

  • Sunday: Sun 5:00–9:30 PM
  • Drive: about 40–45 min from Arden, arrive about 6:45 PM
  • Price: $$$$ · entrées $34–60 · 20% service charge added
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.7★ (3,096)

Lantern-lit garden, red-lacquer dining room, cocktail-chic Palm Beach glam.

Why it fits you two

The most "transported" night on the list. Thai and Pan-Asian under garden lanterns on Palm Beach island, a few blocks from Worth Ave for a stroll after. A great pick for long conversation over shared plates.

What to order

Gluten-free pick
Riceless Seafood Cucumber Roll. Ask for tamari instead of soy sauce.
Caitlin's drink: zero-proof drinks on the menu
Zero-proof list ($12): Lychee Spritz (lychee, white cranberry, lime), Saigon Sunset (watermelon, house infusion) or Tropical Wave. Jasmine Dragon Pearl tea to finish.
Music
No live music listed. Expect a lounge soundtrack and a buzzy bar.

2. Pink Steak

West Palm Beach · SoSo

Modern steakhouse · caviar · raw bar

  • Sunday: Sun dinner 3:30–9:30 PM
  • Drive: about 30–35 min from Arden, arrive about 6:35 PM
  • Price: $$$$ · dishes $16–168
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.6★ (1,235)

Pink-hued, flamingo-glam room with velvet booths and a "Miami meets Palm Beach" energy.

Why it fits you two

The fun, dressed-up, high-energy steak night: wagyu, caviar and a room that feels like a party. It has the most "night out" feeling of the steak options.

What to order

Wagyu Hanger SteakCoffee-rubbed. The Boca Mag favorite and Don's pick

Gluten-free pick
Steaks are naturally GF-friendly. Order the filet with sauces on the side.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published

Music
Sunday's live DJ plays at the Lobsta Disco Brunch (11–3). Dinner still runs lively with music and buzz, so expect a loud room.

3. INTI Peruvian

West Palm Beach · Military Trail

Authentic Peruvian · signature hot-rock dishes

  • Sunday: Sun 1:00–10:00 PM
  • Drive: about 30 min from Arden, arrive about 6:30 PM
  • Price: $$ · OpenTable "$31–50" per person
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.7★ (74)

A strip-mall hidden gem with a moody, gold-lit dining room and art-on-the-plate presentation.

Why it fits you two

This is Caitlin's favorite kind of find: tiny, authentic, beautifully plated, and loved by diners with a 4.7 on OpenTable. It's only about 30 min away, and the kitchen is open until 10, so there's no rush.

What to order

Lomo SaltadoSautéed filet mignon strips, onion, tomato, fries and rice. Don's non-fish pick ($40)
Trilogía de CausasThree causas of chilled whipped aji-amarillo potato. Caitlin's GF-friendly pick ($45)
INTI CarretilleroTheir signature ceviche with crispy seafood and tiger's milk ($36)
Gluten-free pick
Trilogía de Causas (potato-based). Ceviches are citrus-cured. Skip anything fried or crispy unless the kitchen confirms it's GF.
Caitlin's drink: zero-proof drinks on the menu
On the menu: chicha morada (Peru's purple-corn drink, $6), fresh passion fruit juice, or a papaya or strawberry fruit shake. No alcohol needed here.
Music
No live music, just moderate noise and warm music. Room to talk.
"real authentic Peruvian cuisine served at the highest level of quality."OpenTable diner · 4.7★ (74)

Small room, and OpenTable recommends booking. 2800 N Military Trl #103.

4. Okeechobee Steakhouse

West Palm Beach · since 1947

Classic American steakhouse · in-house dry aging

  • Sunday: Sun 4:00–9:00 PM
  • Drive: about 30 min from Arden, arrive about 6:30 PM
  • Price: $$$ · OpenTable "$31–50"+
  • Rating: Resy 4.7★ (8.4K)

Old-school wood-and-leather room, three generations of the Lewis family, and an 800-label wine cellar.

Why it fits you two

Florida's oldest steakhouse has a secret family aging process and old-Florida romance. It's timeless, unhurried and great for talking.

What to order

Dry-Aged Porterhouse for Two (32 oz)Share it. Don's headline non-fish pick
14 oz Delmonico RibeyeA naturally GF-friendly pick with a baked potato and veggies
"Blueberry Dump" dessertBaked blueberries, sugar crust and house gelato. It's a cult favorite
Gluten-free pick
14 oz Delmonico Ribeye with a baked potato and veggies. Tell the server it's for a gluten-free guest.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published
No zero-proof list is published. Ask the bar for a fresh-juice spritz or a virgin build. Sparkling water with lime is always on hand.
Music
No live music listed. It's classic and conversational.
"There's a reason it's been around since 1947."Tripadvisor diner, Sep 13, 2026 · Resy 4.7★ (8.4K)

The kitchen closes at 9, so aim for a 6:30–7:15 seating. 2854 Okeechobee Blvd.

The sunset option. Leave at 6, arrive around 6:50, and catch the 7:03 PM sunset by the lighthouse. Its 4.8 on OpenTable from 2,718 diners is the highest-rated room on the list. It's the farthest drive, so save it for when you want "special."
