Caitlin's checklist

  • Gluten-free: Eddie V's publishes a gluten-sensitive menu. Sunness and Arturo's don't mark GF dishes, so each card names the simplest safe-looking plate. None of these kitchens is dedicated GF, so tell your server and bring your gluten-guard enzyme.
  • Zero-proof: Sunness and Eddie V's print spirit-free drinks on their menus. Arturo's doesn't publish one, and its card says so.
  • Don: a steak or chop at every stop, no fish needed.
  • Dress: business casual works at all three.
Top pick

1. Sunness Supper Club

Fort Lauderdale · East Sunrise Blvd

Modern American supper club · steaks & chops

  • Friday: 5:00–11:00 PM
  • Drive: about 40–50 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:15 PM
  • Price: $$$$ · entrées $25–76 · OpenTable "$50 and over"
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.8★ (465)

Low lights, warm wood and long banquettes downstairs, with Bar Betty, a hidden speakeasy, up the stairs.

Why it fits you two

This is the one with a named act booked for your exact night. Ginetta Vendetta plays pocket trumpet and sings with her jazz combo in the dining room from 6 to 9. After dinner you can head upstairs to Bar Betty, where the Minerva Duo plays from 8:30 to 11:30. Diners give it 4.8 on OpenTable and rate the noise as moderate, so you can still talk.

What to order

Wagyu Beef Skirt Steak & Truffle Frites14 oz with chimichurri. Don's non-fish pick ($76)
Filet Mignon (8 oz)Caitlin's pick, with the demi-glace on the side ($73)
Cabernet Braised Short RibRed bliss mash and honey carrots. Another non-fish pick for Don ($53)
Gluten-free pick
Nothing on the menu is marked GF. The simplest order is the 8 oz filet or the 16 oz Delmonico ribeye with sauce on the side, plus grilled asparagus or broccolini, or the watermelon & feta salad.
Caitlin's drink: zero-proof drinks on the menu
Zero-proof list ($16): the Phony Negroni (The Pathfinder, zero bitter aperitif, zero vermouth) and the Garden Spritz (The Pathfinder, Aplós Calme, lemon, cucumber tonic). Five cocktails marked "R" also come N/A with Ritual, including the Tea Thyme Collins and Elusive Dreams. Two notes: Aplós Calme is hemp-infused, and the Cali Sunrise on that list is a THC drink, so skip that one.
Music
Ginetta Vendetta jazz combo, Fri Oct 9, 6:00–9:00 PM in the dining room. Upstairs at Bar Betty: the Minerva Duo, 8:30–11:30 PM. The club says to call the day of to confirm.
"Every detail was incredible from the secretive entrance, amazing cocktails, food that was unique and delicious, and wonderful service."OpenTable diner · 4.8★ (465)

Valet parking. A 3% adjustment is added when you pay by card. Book for about 6:15 so you hear most of the set. 2465 E Sunrise Blvd.

2. Eddie V's Prime Seafood

Boca Raton · Mizner Park

Steaks & seafood · V Lounge jazz bar

  • Friday: 4:00–10:00 PM, lounge music till 10:30
  • Drive: about 20–25 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:30 PM
  • Price: $$$$ · steaks $62–115
  • Rating: Boca Raton Magazine, reviewed 2024

Chandeliers, white linen and a glass wine tower between the dining room and the V Lounge, where a trio plays every night.

Why it fits you two

The easiest great night: close to home, live jazz, and the most complete gluten-sensitive menu of the three. The steaks are hand-carved in house, and dessert ends with a Bananas Foster butter cake flambéed at the table. Mizner Park is right outside for a walk after.

What to order

Sliced Filet MignonShiitake and oyster mushrooms, tarragon truffle butter. Don's non-fish pick ($65)
10 oz Center Cut Filet MignonOn the gluten-sensitive menu. Caitlin's pick with creamy mashed potatoes and grilled asparagus ($62)
Bananas Foster Butter CakeFlambéed tableside ($16). It has gluten, so this one is Don's
Gluten-free pick
Eddie V's publishes a gluten-sensitive dinner menu: the filet, strip, ribeye, wagyu strip and lamb chops, plus creamy mashed potatoes and grilled asparagus. The kitchen isn't gluten-free, so tell your server.
Caitlin's drink: zero-proof drinks on the menu
Zero-proof "Virtuous" cocktails ($12): the Straight & Narrow (Seedlip Spice 94, hibiscus, lemon, grapefruit soda, basil) and the Rita del Sol (Seedlip Notas de Agave, mango, lime, hot honey, Tajín rim).
Music
John Korba Trio, Fri Oct 9, 6:30–10:30 PM in the V Lounge (Eddie V's Boca October calendar). Sit near the lounge for the music, or in the dining room to talk.
"Few restaurants in the area bestow the gift of nightly live music to their guests, but Eddie V's does."Boca Raton Magazine

Dancing

3. Arturo's Ristorante

Boca Raton · N Federal Hwy

Traditional Italian fine dining · family-run

  • Friday: dinner 5:30–10:00 PM
  • Drive: about 20–25 min from home in Boca, arrive about 7:00 PM
  • Price: $$$ · entrées $36–58 · OpenTable "$31–50"
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.8★ (2,143)

A Tuscan-villa room with white tablecloths, tuxedoed waiters, a dessert cart and a 1,200-label wine cellar.

Why it fits you two

The most romantic, classic night of the three. The same family has run it for generations, there's live piano every night, and couples dance on the main floor. It's about 20 minutes from home and rated 4.8 by more than 2,000 OpenTable diners.

What to order

Filetto di Manzo alla Griglia (8 oz)Grilled filet mignon. Don's non-fish pick ($55)
Pollo PaillardChicken breast pounded and grilled, with chopped salad and tomatoes. Caitlin's GF-friendly pick ($36)
Costoletta di Vitello alla GrigliaGrilled rack of veal chop. Another non-fish pick ($58)
Gluten-free pick
Arturo's says it has gluten-free options but doesn't mark them on the menu. The Pollo Paillard or the grilled filet are the simplest orders. Tell your server.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published
No zero-proof or mocktail list is published. It has a full bar, so ask for something spirit-free. A cappuccino with dessert is on the menu too.
Music
Live piano nightly (OpenTable listing). Boca Raton Magazine says the entertainment runs from jazz piano to Sinatra-style vocals, with dancing on the main floor. Friday's performer isn't posted, so call to ask.
"the atmosphere was beautiful and the father and son musicians made the night very very special."OpenTable diner · 4.8★ (2,143)

Dress is "elegant and sophisticated," smart casual. Free valet. 6750 N Federal Hwy.
