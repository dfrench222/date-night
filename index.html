Caitlin's checklist

  • Gluten-free: Josephine's is the easiest, with gluten-free pasta for any pasta dish. Chez Marie lists gluten-free options, and the simplest order there is duck or filet with potatoes and green beans. Casa Vera has no GF pasta, so stick to grilled lamb or ribeye. None of the three marks GF dishes, so tell your server and bring your gluten-guard enzyme.
  • Zero-proof: None of the three publishes a mocktail or spirit-free list. Josephine's and Casa Vera have full bars, so ask the bartender. Chez Marie pours beer and wine, and its drink list shows sodas, juices, Perrier and iced tea.
  • Don: duck or rack of lamb at Chez Marie, lamb chops or the veal chop at Josephine's, lamb chops or ribeye at Casa Vera. No fish needed anywhere.
  • Music: Neither Chez Marie nor Josephine's posts a Friday lineup online, so ask who's playing when you book.
  • Dress: smart casual works at all three.

1. Chez Marie

Boca Raton · Polo Club Shoppes, Military Trl

Traditional French bistro · family-run, Lyon-trained chef

  • Friday: 5:00–9:30 PM (last seating about 9)
  • Drive: about 10–15 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:15 PM
  • Price: $$–$$$ · OpenTable "$31 to $50" · three-course prix fixe offered
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.6★ (1,137)

White tablecloths, candlelight and a subdued little dining room that reviewers compare to a country bistro in France, plus a covered patio facing the courtyard fountain.

Why it fits you two

The best room of the three for a real conversation. Marie greets guests at the door and her husband, chef Stephane Gattacieca, trained at the Paul Bocuse Institute in Lyon. OpenTable diners give it 4.6 across more than 1,100 reviews, and recent ones say they could hear each other all night. The restaurant's listing adds live music on Fridays from 6 to 9 PM.

What to order

Duck à l'OrangeThe house signature: roasted half duck with orange sauce, garlic-roasted potatoes and green beans. Don's non-fish pick
Filet Mignon8 oz black Angus with peppercorn sauce. Caitlin's simplest GF-friendly order with the sauce on the side
Rack of LambHerbes de Provence, demi-glace, potatoes and green beans. Another non-fish option for Don
Gluten-free pick
OpenTable lists gluten-free options, but nothing on the menu is marked GF. The simplest order is the filet or duck with garlic-roasted potatoes and green beans; ask whether the sauce is thickened with flour and skip the baguette. The website's menu page is an older version with outdated prices, so go by the menu at the table.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published
No zero-proof or mocktail list is published. It pours beer and wine, and its drink list shows sodas, orange or cranberry juice, Perrier and unsweetened iced tea. Ask if they'll mix something spirit-free.
Music
Live music Fridays, 6:00–9:00 PM, per the restaurant's own OpenTable listing. Its website doesn't post a lineup, so call to ask who's playing Oct 23. (The soprano dinner-and-show night is the following Thursday, Oct 29.)
"We wanted to catch up, and we could actually hear! The decor is subdued but perfect, almost like a little place in the French countryside."OpenTable diner, Sept 2026 · 4.6★ (1,137)

Casual to smart casual. Free lot parking at the Polo Club Shoppes off Military Trail and Clint Moore Rd; look for the courtyard to the right of Publix. Reservations are recommended on weekends. 5030 Champion Blvd, Suite D3.

2. Josephine's

Boca Raton · N Federal Hwy, north of Yamato

Sicilian-Italian · family-run since 1990

  • Friday: 5:00–10:00 PM
  • Drive: about 20–25 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:30 PM
  • Price: $$$ · OpenTable "$31 to $50"
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.8★ (898)

Candlelight, white linens, columns and brass chandeliers in the main dining room, plus a garden patio under soft lights and greenery.

Why it fits you two

The dancing pick. Josephine grew up cooking in Sicily and has been part of this Boca room since 1990, and a rotating cast of singers and pianists plays through the week. The restaurant's listing promises music and dancing on Fridays, and OpenTable diners rate it 4.8 across nearly 900 reviews and tag it romantic. One honest note: on packed nights a few diners found the music too loud to talk, so the patio is the place for conversation.

What to order

Lollipop Lamb ChopsGrass-fed, grilled to order, from the chef's favorites. Don's non-fish pick
Prime 9 oz Filet MignonGrilled to order with vegetables and potatoes. Caitlin's simplest GF-friendly order
Any pasta, gluten-freeGF pasta can be swapped into any pasta dish, such as the Rigatoni Bolognese, for a small upcharge
Gluten-free pick
The easiest of the three. Its OpenTable menu says gluten-free or whole-wheat pasta is available for any pasta dish (+$5), and entrées come with vegetables and potatoes. Nothing is marked GF and it isn't listed as celiac-friendly on Find Me Gluten Free, so ask how the GF pasta is cooked and skip breaded dishes like the parmigiana and Francaise.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published
No zero-proof or mocktail list is published. Bar Rafael is a full bar, so ask the bartender for something spirit-free.
Music
Live music and dancing on Fridays, per the restaurant's OpenTable listing (Wednesday, Thursday and Friday). Its website names a rotating lineup (Alexandra Lewis, Giovanni Fazio, Charlie Sullivan, Chenzo Balsamo and Rick Krive) but posts no calendar, so call to ask who's on Oct 23 and when the set starts.

Don's request

3. Casa Vera

Boca Raton · N Federal Hwy

Southern Italian · house-made pasta and Roman pinsa

  • Friday: 12:00–10:00 PM
  • Drive: about 20–25 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:00 PM
  • Price: $$$ · mains $35–65 · OpenTable "$31 to $50"
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.2★ (77)

A bright, white-washed room with blue-and-white tile accents, fresh flowers and a small bar with a view into the kitchen.

Why it fits you two

The one Don wanted to try. Two lifelong friends from Torre del Greco, near the Amalfi Coast, opened it in the past year with house-made pasta and a pinsa dough fermented for 48 hours, and fans say it feels like Sorrento. The catch is service: several recent OpenTable diners waited an hour or more for food on busy nights, including a Friday, which is why it sits at 4.2. Go early and treat it as a long, unhurried dinner.

What to order

Costolette di AgnelloGrilled lamb chops with aromatic herbs. Don's non-fish pick ($55)
Ribeye di ManzoGrilled premium ribeye. Caitlin's simplest GF-friendly order with grilled asparagus or mashed potatoes ($48)
Pappardelle alla BologneseSlow-cooked veal and beef ragù. Another non-fish option for Don ($32)
Gluten-free pick
The hardest of the three for gluten-free. No GF pasta is listed, and the pastas and pinsa are wheat. The simplest order is the ribeye or lamb chops with grilled asparagus ($12) or mashed potatoes ($12). Tell your server up front and ask about any sauce.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published
No zero-proof or mocktail list is published. A recent diner praised the cocktails from its small bar, so ask the bartender for something spirit-free.
Music
No live music is listed on its website or OpenTable. Reviewers describe a lively, sometimes loud room on Friday nights.
"Finally an Italian restaurant in Boca where you feel like you could be in Sorrento."OpenTable diner, Sept 2026 · 4.2★ (77)

Business casual. Book a 6:00 table, since reviewers say the kitchen backs up later on busy nights. OpenTable lists no parking details, so ask when you book. 3249 N Federal Hwy.

After dinner

Stay for a dance

At Josephine's, finish dinner on the patio, then head inside when the music picks up and take a turn on the floor.

A fountain and dessert

At Chez Marie, save room for the lavender crème brûlée (ask whether it's gluten-free), then take a slow lap past the courtyard fountain outside.

Home early

All three are within about 25 minutes of home, so the night can end on your own couch well before ten.
