Caitlin's checklist

  • Gluten-free: None of the three marks GF dishes. The simplest orders are the filet or half chicken at Casablanca, the herb chicken or ribeye with asparagus at Flare House, and the filet or Chicken Dijonnaise with sides of asparagus or spinach at Café L'Europe. Ask about sauces, tell your server, and bring your gluten-guard enzyme.
  • Zero-proof: Casablanca publishes three spirit-free cocktails at $12, plus Heineken 0.0. Flare House and Café L'Europe don't post one, so ask the bartender.
  • Don: filet, half chicken or short rib pappardelle at Casablanca; lamb chops, ribeye or oxtails at Flare House; filet au poivre, NY strip, short ribs or schnitzel at Café L'Europe. No fish needed anywhere.
  • Music: Casablanca's piano starts at 7:30 and Flare House's band at 8. Café L'Europe doesn't post music online, so ask when booking.
  • Dress: smart casual at Casablanca and Flare House. Café L'Europe is dressier: no caps, and most men wear a jacket.
Oceanfront + zero-proof list

1. Casablanca Cafe

Fort Lauderdale Beach · Alhambra St at A1A

Mediterranean-American · in a 1927 villa

  • Friday: Open until 11 PM Friday
  • Drive: about 35–40 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:30 PM
  • Price: $$–$$$ · mains mostly $29–$49
  • Rating: Fort Lauderdale Magazine's 2026 Best Beachfront Restaurant

A Mediterranean-style villa from 1927 sitting right across from the ocean, with an upstairs dining room, a balcony and a piano bar downstairs.

Why it fits you two

It covers the most of what you two like in one place: an ocean view, live piano on Friday, a real zero-proof cocktail list for Caitlin, and plenty of non-fish mains for Don. It's the most relaxed of the three and still feels like a night out.

What to order

Filet Mignon8 oz with blue cheese gratin, creamed spinach and red wine sauce, $49. Don's non-fish pick, or Caitlin's with the sauce on the side
Roasted Half ChickenMoroccan mezze rice bowl with chickpeas, baba ghanoush and tzatziki, $29
Short Rib PappardelleBraised short rib over pappardelle, $32. Another non-fish option for Don
Gluten-free pick
Nothing on the menu is marked gluten-free. The simplest orders are the filet or half chicken (ask about the sauces and the gratin), the Greek salad without pita, and sides of confetti rice or garlic spinach. Tell your server.
Caitlin's drink: zero-proof drinks on the menu
Published zero-proof list, $12 each: Alhambra Spritz (rum alternative, strawberry, blueberry, citrus, ginger beer, mint), Sebastian Sunrise (gin alternative, hibiscus, lemon, elderflower) and Casa Smash (tequila alternative, blueberry, raspberry, citrus, agave, San Pellegrino). Heineken 0.0 too.
Music
Live piano Fridays from 7:30 PM (The Beth and John Show), per the restaurant's live music page.
"2026 Best Beachfront Restaurant"Fort Lauderdale Magazine, as shown on Casablanca's website

No reservations. Join the online waitlist on the way so your wait is shorter. Valet from 4 PM on Fridays. Smart casual. 3049 Alhambra St, Fort Lauderdale.

Live band + dancing

2. Flare House

West Palm Beach · Northwood Village

American fusion supper club

  • Friday: 4:00 PM–1:00 AM Friday
  • Drive: about 45 min from home in Boca, arrive about 7:00 PM
  • Price: $$–$$$ · mains mostly $25–$45
  • Rating: Mixed-to-good guest reviews

A low-lit Northwood dining room with purple lighting, a stage and live music every Friday night.

Why it fits you two

This is the dancing night. The FM Band plays R&B and soul at 8 PM on Oct 30, with a $6 cover, and Friday music runs until 1 AM. Have dinner first, then stay for the set.

What to order

Lollipop LambFive chops with chimichurri and two sides, $45. Don's non-fish pick
Flame-Grilled Herb ChickenWith asparagus and yellow rice, $25. Caitlin's simplest GF-friendly order
10 oz Boneless Ribeye$39. Another easy non-fish main
Gluten-free pick
Nothing is marked gluten-free. The simplest order is the herb chicken or ribeye with asparagus or yellow rice. Ask how the meat is seasoned and tell your server.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published
The drinks menu isn't published online, so there's no zero-proof list to point to. It has a full bar, so ask for a mocktail.
Music
The FM Band (R&B and soul) at 8:00 PM on Fri Oct 30, $6 cover, per Flare House's entertainment page. Live Music Fridays run 4 PM to 1 AM.

Classic Palm Beach glamour

3. Café L'Europe

Palm Beach · South County Rd

French-inspired bistro and grand bar · family-run since 1980

  • Friday: Dinner from 5 PM
  • Drive: about 45 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:45 PM
  • Price: $$$$ · mains mostly $53–$79
  • Rating: A Palm Beach institution since 1980

Navy awnings, white-tablecloth dining rooms with a grand piano, a big bar and candlelit outdoor tables on County Road.

Why it fits you two

The dressy, grown-up choice. The Marcello family has run it since 1980, the room is made for a long conversation, and the menu has plenty of steak and chicken for Don. It's the priciest of the three.

What to order

Filet Mignon au Poivre8 oz USDA filet with potato gratin and spinach, $79. Don's non-fish pick
Chicken DijonnaisePan-roasted Bell & Evans chicken, mashed potato, green beans, $61
Braised Short RibsWith mashed potatoes and cabernet wine sauce, $64
Gluten-free pick
The menu doesn't mark gluten-free dishes. The simplest order is the filet or NY strip with a side of grilled asparagus or sautéed spinach, sauce on the side. Ask when you book and tell your server.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published
No zero-proof or mocktail list is published. It has a grand bar, so ask the bartender to make something spirit-free.
Music
Its website doesn't post live music, though there's a grand piano in the dining room and a third-party listing mentions a pianist. Ask when booking whether someone is playing Friday. (Its Halloween party with a DJ is Saturday, Oct 31.)
"French bistro, grand bar, outdoor dining, private dining room"Café L'Europe's own website

Reservations on Resy. Complimentary valet. Dress up a little: no caps, and most men wear a jacket. 331 S County Rd, Palm Beach.
