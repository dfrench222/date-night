Caitlin's checklist

  • Gluten-free: None of the three marks GF dishes or runs a dedicated GF kitchen. Bourbon Steak is the strongest (dedicated fryer, a chef who takes allergies seriously). Café Vico has a gluten-free cake on its dessert menu. CVI.CHE is the most limited because of its sauces. Tell your server and bring your gluten-guard enzyme.
  • Zero-proof: Bourbon Steak and CVI.CHE print spirit-free cocktails on their menus. Café Vico doesn't publish one, and its card says so.
  • Don: lamb chops, steaks or brick chicken at every stop, no fish needed.
  • Phones-off Friday: book the table, then leave both phones in the glovebox.
  • Dress: smart casual works at all three.

1. Café Vico

Fort Lauderdale · N Federal Hwy

Northern Italian supper club · family-run 30+ years

  • Friday: 4:00–10:00 PM (OpenTable says 11)
  • Drive: about 40–50 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:00 PM
  • Price: $$$ · entrées $30–60 · OpenTable "$31 to $50"
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.8★ (3,145)

Low light, white tablecloths, a painted-sky ceiling with a chandelier, and signed Hollywood photos on nearly every wall. The piano bar has a small dance floor.

Why it fits you two

The warmest, most welcome-home room of the three. Marco and Claire Vico have run it for more than 30 years and still greet guests at the door, Danny DelRay plays the piano bar on Fridays, and there's room to dance if the song is right. OpenTable diners give it 4.8 across more than 3,000 reviews and rate the noise as moderate.

What to order

Lamb Chops GrilledFine herbs and garlic. Don's non-fish pick ($49.95)
New York StripCaitlin's simplest GF-friendly order, plain, with broccoli or mashed potato on the side ($58.95)
Gluten Free CakeOn the dessert menu, so Caitlin gets dessert too ($9.95)
Gluten-free pick
OpenTable lists gluten-free options and the dessert menu has a gluten-free cake, but no entrées are marked GF. The simplest order is the grilled NY strip or lamb chops with plain broccoli or mashed potato and no sauce. One recent diner said the in-house menu has changed from the website, so confirm with your server.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published
No zero-proof or mocktail list is published. It has a full bar, so ask the bartender for something spirit-free.
Music
Danny DelRay at the piano bar, Fri Oct 16. Café Vico's events calendar lists him 5:30–7:30 PM; his own site says a 7 PM showtime, with requests welcome (Sinatra, Dean Martin, pop). The site also says live music runs Fridays and Saturdays from 5 PM to close. Call to confirm the set times.
"We asked for a quiet table on this busy evening and got exactly that. A perfect, relaxed dinner after a busy day."OpenTable diner · 4.8★ (3,145)

Smart casual. Strip-mall parking on site, half a block north of Sunrise Blvd. Happy hour runs until 7 PM. Sit near the piano bar for the music, or ask for the main dining room or patio to talk. 1125 N Federal Hwy.

2. Bourbon Steak

Delray Beach · The Seagate, Atlantic Ave

Michael Mina steakhouse · wood-fire grill

  • Friday: 5:00–10:00 PM
  • Drive: about 30–35 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:30 PM
  • Price: $$$$ · steaks $62–105 · OpenTable "$50 and over"
  • Rating: OpenTable 4.8★ (137)

Leather chairs, warm wood and a swirling green carpet under brass chandeliers, inside The Seagate Hotel at the beach end of Atlantic Avenue.

Why it fits you two

The most polished of the three, and the strongest for Caitlin's two must-haves: a printed zero-proof list and a kitchen celiac diners trust. Michael Mina's steaks are poached in butter and finished over oak, hickory and cherrywood, and the signature beignets come with three dipping custards. OpenTable diners tag it romantic. One honest note: there's no live music on Friday.

What to order

8 oz Center-Cut Filet MignonCaitlin's pick, with potato purée or a baked potato. Ask which sauces are GF ($62)
16 oz Delmonico RibeyeOff the wood-fire grill. Don's non-fish pick ($82)
Free-Range Brick ChickenSweet and spicy peppers, chili, oregano. Another non-fish option ($44)
Gluten-free pick
Nothing is marked GF on the menu. A celiac reviewer on Find Me Gluten Free says most dishes can be made gluten-free, the chef takes allergies seriously, and the fries come from a dedicated fryer. Simplest order: the filet with potato purée or a baked potato. Tell your server when you sit down.
Caitlin's drink: zero-proof drinks on the menu
Two non-alcoholic cocktails on the current list ($16): Our Little Secret (Lyre's Italian Spritz, lemon, passion fruit, Fever-Tree ginger beer) and Easy Money (Lyre's Coffee, Lavazza espresso, vanilla demerara). Easy Money has espresso in it, so it's the caffeine pick.
Music
No live music on Friday. The restaurant lists live music on the patio on Thursdays only, 6:30–9:30 PM. One recent OpenTable diner found the dining-room playlist loud, so ask for a quieter table or the patio.
"The food was amazing, Andrew V provided excellent service, and the onsite valet was very convenient for a Saturday night in Delray."OpenTable diner, anniversary dinner · 4.8★ (137)

Complimentary valet. Smart casual. Book around 6:30 to catch the last light, since sunset is 6:52. 1000 E Atlantic Ave at Venetian Dr.

Closest

3. CVI.CHE 105

Boca Raton · Town Center

Peruvian · ceviche, lomo saltado, Nikkei

  • Friday: 11:30 AM–11:00 PM
  • Drive: about 15–20 min from home in Boca, arrive about 7:00 PM
  • Price: $$$ · mains $24–75
  • Rating: Boca Raton Magazine, reviewed July 2025

Peacocks perched in greenery overhead, a kaleidoscopic textile installation, green velvet bar stools and a busy patio.

Why it fits you two

The easy one: about 15 minutes from home, the Peruvian food Caitlin loves, and four zero-proof cocktails on the menu. Chef-owner Juan Chipoco's Miami group brought it to Boca in 2025, and Don gets classic lomo saltado or a prime ribeye. Fair warning: the room is lively and colorful with upbeat background music, so it's the least quiet of the three.

What to order

Lomo SaltadoWok-seared tenderloin, onion, tomato, fries and white rice. Don's non-fish pick ($26)
Ribeye al Grill16 oz prime ribeye, truffle potato purée, broccolini. Caitlin's simplest GF-friendly order; confirm the purée ($75)
Anticuchos, filet mignonGrilled skewers in anticuchera sauce. A non-fish starter for Don ($27)
Gluten-free pick
This is the hardest of the three for gluten-free. There's no GF menu, and a celiac diner on Find Me Gluten Free was told most dishes have gluten in the sauces. The staff knew their stuff and offered grilled meat, fish or vegetables with plain rice, and the classic ceviche worked. Lomo saltado is usually made with soy sauce, so it isn't a safe pick for Caitlin.
Caitlin's drink: zero-proof drinks on the menu
Non-alcoholic cocktails on the menu: Lima en el Coco ($10: cranberry, lime, rosemary, coconut water), Pachamango ($10: lime, mango, coriander seeds, soda), Virgin Mule ($9) and Virgin Mojito ($9), each with mango, strawberry, passion fruit or coconut for +$2. This is the group-wide drinks menu, so confirm Boca pours all four.
Music
No live music is listed. Expect upbeat recorded music; Boca Raton Magazine's reviewer heard a club remix of "Sweet Dreams" during dinner. Ask for a patio table if you want it quieter.
"CVI.CHE 105 has brought a lively Latin spirit to Boca, and I'm here for it."Boca Raton Magazine

Mall lot parking. Book on SevenRooms and choose the Boca Raton location. The photos come from the CVI.CHE 105 group site and may show a sister location. 6000 Glades Rd, Suite 1230.
