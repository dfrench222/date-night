1. Swifty's at The Colony

Palm Beach · Hammon Ave, just off Worth Avenue

Classic American supper club by the pool

  • Friday: Dinner 5–10 PM; live music poolside 6–10 PM
  • Drive: about 50 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:45 PM
  • Price: $$$$ · OpenTable lists $50 and over; the online menu has no prices
  • Rating: 4.6 on OpenTable from 642 reviews (older reviews; booking is on Resy now)

A romantic indoor-outdoor room around The Colony's pool, under a lush, twinkling hanging garden, with a classic, clubby bar and lounge inside.

Why it fits you two

You've been on the road, so this one feels like a proper welcome home. A guitarist or band by the pool, Caitlin's drinks spelled out on the menu, and a filet for Don. Dress up a little and make it a night.

What to order

8 oz Prime Filet Mignon (GF)With Swifty's fries, grilled tomato, jumbo asparagus and peppercorn sauce, chimichurri or truffle butter. Don's non-fish pick
Bacon-Wrapped Wagyu MeatloafMashed potato, roast carrots, sautéed spinach. Another non-fish option
Bell & Evans Chicken Paillard (GF)Lemon, shoestring potatoes, arugula, tomato and parmesan salad. An easy GF order for Caitlin
Gluten-free pick
Swifty's marks GF dishes on its menu: the filet, 14 oz prime skirt steak, 12 oz Wagyu NY strip, chicken paillard, artichoke, oysters and shrimp. The kitchen handles wheat, so tell your server it's for a gluten sensitivity and ask about the fries.
Caitlin's drink: zero-proof drinks on the menu
Swifty's prints Spirit Free Sips: the Royal Palm Paloma (passion fruit, agave, lime, Seedlip Grove, grapefruit soda), the Ocean View (Seedlip Garden, cucumber, lime, jalapeño, mint, agave, ginger ale) and a French Spritz with French Bloom non-alcoholic sparkling wine. Stella Artois 0.0 is on the beer list too.
Music
Live music poolside, Friday 6–10 PM, plus Living Room entertainment Nov 26–28, 5:30–8:30 PM, per The Colony's dining page. The performer isn't named, so call (561) 655-5430 to ask who's playing.
"They also had entertainment in the way of a guitarist and it added to the ambience."OpenTable diner review

Book on Resy and ask for a table outside by the music. Valet only, or street parking. Resort casual. During the Mar-a-Lago visit, A1A is closed between South County Rd and Southern Blvd, so take the Okeechobee Blvd or Flagler bridge. 155 Hammon Ave, Palm Beach.

2. Blue Jean Blues

Fort Lauderdale · NE 33rd St

Jazz and blues club with American bar food

  • Friday: Open 4 PM–2 AM; V-Connection 6:30–10 PM
  • Drive: about 35 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:15 PM
  • Price: $$ · burgers, sandwiches and pizza $17–$30; $5 per table for the show

A small, friendly club lit in blue neon, with local bands most nights and a little dance floor. Friday's show is set up outdoors.

Why it fits you two

This is the dancing night. V-Connection plays R&B, pop and dance, so you'll know the songs, and the room is small enough that nobody's shy for long. Dinner is easy bar food, so the music is the main event.

What to order

1/2 Pound Burger$19. Angus beef, lettuce, tomato, pickles, fries. Don's non-fish pick
Grilled Chicken Breast Sandwich$19. Cheddar, bacon, lettuce and tomato. Another non-fish option
Personal Margherita Pizza, gluten-free crust$21 with a side salad. Mozzarella, tomato and basil. Caitlin's GF order
Gluten-free pick
The menu offers a gluten-free personal pan pizza crust. Nothing else is marked GF, and a lot of the menu is fried, so keep it to the GF pizza or a salad without croutons and ask your server.
Caitlin's drink: no zero-proof list published
No mocktail list is published. The beer list includes Stella N/A, and the bar can make a mocktail if you ask.
Music
V-Connection, Friday Nov 27, 6:30–10 PM, listed on the club's own calendar as R&B, pop and dance, a Friday night outdoor event with $5 tables.
"Small but so welcoming and great people with good live music."Google review shown on bluejeanblues.live

Reserve a table on the club's site by 2 PM Friday. Tables are held 15 minutes after the 6:30 start, and paid tables can't be refunded inside 72 hours. The show is outdoors, so bring a light layer. 3320 NE 33rd St, Fort Lauderdale.

Closest + mostly gluten-free

3. Farmer's Table

Boca Raton · N Military Trail at NW 19th St

Farm-to-table, clean-eating American

  • Friday: Dinner 5–9 PM
  • Drive: about 15 min from home in Boca, arrive about 6:30 PM
  • Price: $$ · mains $25–$42; OpenTable lists $30 and under
  • Rating: 4.6 on OpenTable from 6,714 reviews

Warm and inviting, with a patio out back by the hotel pool. The dining room can get lively at dinner, per OpenTable diners.

Why it fits you two

If the trip left you tired, this is the low-effort win. Caitlin can order almost anything on the menu, the zero-proof elixirs are a real list, and you're home by nine.

What to order

Grass-Fed Short Rib (GF)$42. Roasted root vegetables and mushrooms, mashed sweet potatoes, natural jus. Don's non-fish pick
"French Dip" Wagyu Beef Brochette (GF)$39. Potato skins, broccolini, horseradish sauce, onion au jus. Another non-fish option
"Spasta" Chicken Meatballs (GF)$26. Over roasted spaghetti squash with spinach and pomodoro. A light GF order for Caitlin
Gluten-free pick
Most of the dinner menu is marked GF, including the short rib, the brochette, the chicken meatballs, the salmon, branzino and every side. Flatbreads can go on a GF cauliflower crust for $4. The menu notes they're not a gluten-free facility, so mention it to your server.
Caitlin's drink: zero-proof drinks on the menu
Seven zero-proof elixirs at $11, all marked GF: The Essential (beet, carrot, apple, ginger, berries), Cuke of Earl, What's Up Doc, The Remedy (green tea, pomegranate, berries, mint), Pineapple Express, Kiwi Refresher and Butterfly Effect (grapefruit, passion fruit, kombucha, butterfly tea).
Music
No live music is posted for Friday. OpenTable says live music is often featured on weekend evenings, so call (561) 417-5836 if you want to know.
"Celebrated our anniversary there and staff made it so special. Food was delicious."OpenTable diner review, Nov 2026

Book on OpenTable and ask for the patio. Free on-site parking. Casual dress. Thanksgiving prix fixe ends Thursday, so Friday is the regular menu. 1901 N Military Trail, Boca Raton.
